Kharcha privacy

Kharcha is an expense tracker. This page says what it reads, what it keeps, and how to stop it. Updated 19 November 2026.

Where your data lives

  • Your expenses stay on your device: in the Android app, or in your browser for the web app.
  • There is no Kharcha account and no Kharcha server. Nothing is sold or shared.
  • If you set up the optional Kharcha bot, it runs on your own computer and keeps a copy there.

Gmail receipts (optional)

  • If you turn it on, Kharcha asks Google for read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly).
  • It only opens emails from the shops you pick, such as Swiggy, Zomato or Uber, and reads the amount, shop and date.
  • It never sends, deletes, moves or changes emails, and never marks them as read.
  • The email itself isn't saved. Only the expense it becomes is kept, on your device.
  • The Google access token stays in your browser's memory or in Android's own Google sign-in. It is never sent anywhere else.

Google API data

Kharcha's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to add your own expenses, is not used for ads, is not transferred to anyone, and is not read by people.

How to stop it

  • In Kharcha: Import → Gmail receipts → Disconnect.
  • In your Google Account: Third-party connections → Kharcha → Delete all connections.
  • To erase everything Kharcha keeps: More → Delete all data.
